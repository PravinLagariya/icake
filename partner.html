Business Partnership Opportunities

Irresistible Cakes (iCakes) is inviting applications for business partnerships across Ontario. If you're interested in building a business around a well-established cake and dessert brand, we'd like to hear from you.

Two Ways to Partner

There are two formats to choose from, depending on the kind of business you want to build.

  • Custom Cakes — a made-to-order business built around custom cake sales, with pickup as the primary way customers receive their order.
  • Dessert Café — a dine-in space where customers can enjoy desserts alongside coffee, tea, or ice cream, plus takeout of ready-made items and custom orders for parties and events.

What a Dessert Café Offers

A Dessert Café location typically carries a broader everyday menu than a Custom Cakes location, built around dine-in and takeout together:

  • Cakes & Custom Orders — display cakes, cake slices, and custom orders for any party or occasion
  • Baked Goods & Pastries — pastries, muffins, scones, cream rolls, brownies, cupcakes, cookies, and donuts
  • Specialty Desserts — mousse cups including triple chocolate, strawberry, and mango, cake balls, and milk cakes
  • Beverages — coffee, tea, hot chocolate, milkshakes, smoothies, and ice cream in vanilla and chocolate

Is This Opportunity Right for You?

This partnership works well for people who:

  • Want to run a retail or business operation, without needing to bake themselves
  • Are comfortable managing a team and handling day-to-day customer service
  • Know their local market and community well
  • Are ready to invest both time and capital into building a business
  • Would rather start with existing training, support, and brand recognition than build something from scratch entirely

What Makes Irresistible Cakes (iCakes) the Right Partner

When you partner with iCakes, you're building on a name that already carries weight. We've been part of Ontario's cake and dessert industry since 1962 — over six decades of experience that customers recognize and trust.

The brand already operates 10 pickup locations across the GTA, and our dedicated nut-free facility has helped build a reputation for quality and dietary care that would otherwise take new business years to establish on its own.

You're not introducing an unfamiliar name to your market — you're extending one people may already know.

What We Offer Partners

Low Set-Up Costs & Low Overheads

Starting a new business often comes with high upfront costs. We've structured our partnership model to help keep your initial investment and ongoing overheads manageable, so more of your revenue can go toward growing your business rather than covering fixed costs.

Full Training & Support

You don't need to come in as a cake industry expert. Our team provides hands-on training covering day-to-day operations, and you'll also have access to an experienced decorator to help maintain the quality and presentation our brand is known for.

Help Choosing the Right Location

Where you set up matters. We support you with demographic analysis to help identify a location with strong potential, and help you prepare the space for opening.

A Ready-to-Launch Package

We aim to make the process of opening your business as straightforward as possible, with a package designed to help you move from initial agreement to opening day efficiently.

Operational Tools From Day One

Partners get access to an online ordering platform and a modern phone system, so your business has the tools it needs to operate smoothly from the start, without having to build these systems yourself.

What You Bring as a Partner

A partnership works both ways. Alongside the support we provide, partners are generally expected to bring:

  • Capital investment for setup, based on your specific location and business plan
  • Day-to-day management of the business, including operations and customer experience
  • Local staffing — hiring, training, and managing your own team
  • A commitment to keeping quality and presentation consistent with the Irresistible Cakes brand
  • Local marketing and community engagement, working alongside the support we provide centrally

How Costs & Operations Work, By Location

The way a partnership operates — and what it costs — depends heavily on where your business is located, so we want to be upfront about this before you reach out.

Within the GTA

Your business receives cakes and desserts ready-made from our central bakery, so you won't need your own baking setup or expertise. Because production is centralized, equipment and setup costs are typically lower, and your focus stays on sales, customer service, and presentation.

If you need a full setup in your facility, our team is happy to help.

Outside the GTA

Since central delivery isn't practical at greater distances, partners outside the GTA generally bake on-site as part of running the business. This brings additional equipment, training, and setup costs into the picture, on top of the standard costs that apply everywhere — things like location setup, initial inventory, and day-to-day operating expenses.

Because these details vary so much by location, our team will walk you through exactly what applies to your situation during our first conversation, rather than publish generic numbers that might not reflect your actual costs.

The Partnership Process

  1. Initial Inquiry — Reach out to our team and share a bit about yourself and your goals.
  2. Introductory Discussion — We'll walk you through the partnership model and what's expected on both sides.
  3. Location Review — If applicable, we support a location evaluation, including demographic analysis to help find a strong site.
  4. Agreement — Once terms are agreed, we finalize the partnership agreement together.
  5. Training — You'll receive hands-on training, plus support from an experienced decorator, to get ready for opening.
  6. Launch — Your business opens with tools like our online ordering platform and phone system ready from day one.

Ongoing Support After You Open

Support doesn't end once your business opens. Partners continue to receive:

  • Access to an experienced decorator for ongoing quality and presentation support
  • Product and menu updates as the Irresistible Cakes range evolves
  • Continued use of the online ordering platform and phone system
  • An ongoing point of contact with our team for questions as your business grows
