Shipping & Delivery Policy

Irresistible Cakes (iCakes) delivers custom cakes across the GTA and select surrounding regions for customers who'd rather not collect their order in person. Delivery method, timing, and terms all depend on the type of cake ordered and its destination — a reflection of how delicate and individually made each cake is. Please review this policy before choosing delivery, so there are no surprises on the day.

1. Delivery Days & Timeframes

Delivery availability differs by cake type. Here's how the week breaks down:

Monday

Small & 2-Tier Cakes Regular deliveries are not available. Special delivery arrangements are available
Large & Wedding Cakes Special deliveries are available. 3 hour delivery window, confirmed at time of order

Tuesday To Sunday

Small & 2-Tier Cakes Orders are delivered by a third-party courier between 11:00 AM and 6:00 PM. Specific delivery times cannot be guaranteed.
Large & Wedding Cakes 3 hour delivery window, confirmed at time of order

Statutory Holidays

Small & 2-Tier Cakes Regular deliveries are not available. Special delivery arrangements are available.
Large & Wedding Cakes 3 hour delivery window, confirmed at time of order

3. Third-Party Delivery for Smaller Orders

In general, orders under $350, delivered within the areas covered by our pickup locations, go out through a third-party courier on our behalf, at a competitive delivery rate.

A few things worth knowing when a third party is handling your delivery:

  • Once your order is handed off, the courier's service is outside our control.
  • Irresistible Cakes (iCakes) isn't liable for delivery issues or transport damage in these cases.
  • Because of that, picking up your cake in person remains the safer bet whenever it's an option — no third-party handling, no guesswork about timing.

Orders of $350 or more go through our premium delivery service instead — get in touch with our team when placing the order to lock in the details.

4. Failed Delivery Attempts

If no one is available to accept the cake when the driver arrives, the courier is allowed to dispose of it. Unfortunately, this means you will not receive a refund.

To help make sure this doesn't happen, here's what you can do:

  • Have someone available at the delivery address during the confirmed delivery timeframe.
  • Let us know ahead of time about a gate code, buzzer number, or anything else the driver might need to reach the door.
  • Give clear directions if the entrance is hard to find, such as a side door or a specific building entrance.
  • Share a backup phone number in case the driver needs to reach someone quickly.

Taking these steps goes a long way toward making sure your cake arrives safely, on time, and in the right hands.

5. Why Pickup Is Worth Considering

Third-party delivery comes with real trade-offs: no fixed arrival time, no liability coverage if something goes wrong in transit, and the risk of losing the order entirely if delivery falls through.

Picking up your cake sidesteps all of that — you see it, you carry it, you know exactly how it's handled from bakery to table. iCakes operates 10 pickup locations across the GTA, so there's likely one close to you.
