FREE Cake Sampling and Consultation in Woodbridge

If you're planning a wedding cake or any large custom order, we'd genuinely recommend booking a FREE Cake Sampling and Consultation at the Woodbridge showroom before you lock anything in.

During the consultation, you can talk through flavour and filling combinations, look at design ideas, and discuss the size and style that make sense for your guest count — since a cake that looks right in a photo is sometimes wrong for a room of 150 people. It's simply time set aside to figure out what actually works for your event, before anything is finalized.

Note: FREE Cake Sampling and Consultation appointments are available at our showroom on Tuesday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday. Appointment times vary by day, so check the booking page for current availability.

Baked Safe, Baked Right

100% Nut-Free Facility

The entire production facility is 100% nut-free, with no peanuts or tree nuts used in any product. That's not an add-on or a special request — it's the baseline for everything made here, which is worth knowing if you're ordering for a school event, a daycare, or any setting with allergy considerations.

Halal, Alcohol-Free & Gelatine-Free

Every cake is halal, alcohol-free, and gelatine-free as standard. Eggless, vegan, and gluten-free options are available for selected cakes — the team can explain the available options and how dietary changes may affect flavour and texture.

HalalAlcohol-FreeGelatine-FreeEgglessVeganGluten-Free

Lactose-Free Options

Lactose-free options are also available, although availability can vary by cake and flavour. If lactose-free is required for your order, mention it when placing your order so the team can confirm the available options.
