Our Heart Shape Full Face Photo Birthday Cake is the ultimate personalized centerpiece for birthdays, anniversaries, and romantic celebrations across Toronto and the GTA. Featuring a high-resolution edible image printed directly onto your cake face, framed by custom piping and border trim colors of your choice.

Baked fresh daily in our dedicated 100% nut-free facility using real custard cream and premium sponge cake. Halal and lactose-free as standard, with eggless, vegan, and gluten-free options available.

Standard lead time is 2 to 3 days for custom photo cakes. Same-day pickup may be available at our Woodbridge main showroom for select ready-made designs. GTA doorstep delivery is available Tuesday through Sunday.

Single-tier heart shape sponge cake with custard cream filling, edible photo icing sheet, and fresh buttercream border piping.

Refrigeration required immediately upon pickup or delivery (1 to 3 degrees C). Keeps fresh up to 5 days under proper refrigeration. Do not freeze edible photo sheets.
