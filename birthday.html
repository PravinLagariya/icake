Dietary Options

Nut-Free, Eggless, Gluten-Free & More

Every birthday cake at iCakes is made in a 100% nut-free facility as standard — no special request needed. This applies to every design, every flavour, and every order size.

Additional dietary options available across most of the range:

100% Nut-Free Facility

The entire baking environment — every surface, tool, and ingredient — is completely nut-free. Not a nut-free product from a shared kitchen, but a nut-free bakery where nuts have never been present. Our allergen-safe cakes are trusted across Brampton and the wider GTA.

Learn more

Lactose-Free

Lactose-free, dairy-free-friendly milk is used as a core ingredient across most flavours — built into the original recipe rather than offered as a substitution. Most guests with lactose intolerance need no modification to the standard order.

Learn more

Eggless & Vegan

Eggless cakes are made on dedicated egg-free recipes across 13+ flavours, not a standard recipe with eggs removed. Vegan cakes are also available on special order across the GTA — mark the requirement at order time.

Learn more

Gluten-Free

A dedicated gluten-free sponge base, not a flour substitution applied to the standard recipe. Available across select flavours on special order — confirm the requirement when placing your order.

Learn more

Halal Friendly

All products are halal, alcohol-free, and gelatine-free as standard, including every flavouring agent. The vanilla used across the range contains no alcohol, so each product qualifies without a separate request.

Learn more
