Customer Policy

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Irresistible Cakes (iCakes) collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information in the course of running our business — through our website, over the phone, by email, and in person. It applies to anyone who places an order, creates an account, submits a contact form, or otherwise shares personal information with us.

As our business or our obligations under Canadian privacy law evolve, we'll update this page and revise that date accordingly, so it's worth checking back periodically if privacy matters to you.

Information We Collect

We collect a limited set of personal information, organized here by category so it's clear exactly what we hold and why.

Identity Data

Your full name, delivery or pickup address, email address, and phone number, collected when you place an order, book an appointment, submit a contact or inquiry form, or otherwise reach out to us. If you contact us directly, this may also include the subject and content of your message. For certain large or custom orders, we may also request reasonable identification at pickup to verify the person authorized to collect the order.

Financial Data

Payment for online orders is processed through Moneris, a secure, independent third-party payment platform. We never see or store your complete credit card number — only confirmation that your payment was successful.

Technical Data

When you browse our website, certain information is captured automatically: your IP address, browser type, the device you're using, and tracking identifiers used by our analytics and advertising tools.

How We Collect Your Data

Direct Interaction

Most of the information we hold comes directly from you: filling out an order form, requesting a quote for a custom cake, creating an account, submitting a contact or inquiry form, or subscribing to receive offers and updates from us.

Automated Technologies

The rest is gathered automatically as you browse our site, through browser cookies and analytics tags. This includes tools such as Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, which help us understand how visitors use our website, and Meta Pixel, which measures the performance of our advertising on Facebook and Instagram. You can limit or disable these through your browser's cookie settings at any time.

How We Use Your Information

We collect personal information for specific, limited purposes:


  • To manage, prepare, and fulfill your custom bakery order, from confirmation through pickup or delivery.
  • To send order confirmations, pickup reminders, and delivery updates related to an active order.
  • To respond to questions, quote requests, and customer service inquiries, including those submitted through our contact form.
  • To send promotional offers and updates — strictly on an opt-in basis, and only to customers who've actively consented to receive them.

We don't use your information for purposes beyond these without asking you first.

SMS & Text Message Consent

If you've provided your phone number for order notifications or marketing texts, here's what governs that specifically:

  • Phone numbers collected for SMS purposes are used only by iCakes for the communications you've agreed to receive — they are not shared with outside parties for their own marketing use.
  • Transactional messages (order confirmations, pickup reminders, delivery updates) and marketing messages (promotions, offers) are treated as separate categories of consent. Marketing texts are sent only if you've specifically opted in to receive them.
  • You can opt out at any time. Reply STOP to unsubscribe from text messages, or HELP if you need assistance. Commercial electronic messages, including marketing emails and text messages, are sent in accordance with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), including applicable consent, identification, and unsubscribe requirements.

The full consent terms you agree to when placing an order, including message frequency and applicable message and data rates, are set out in our Terms & Conditions at the point of ordering.

We don't sell, rent, or trade your information to third-party advertisers or data brokers, ever. To actually get your cake to you and run our business, though, a limited amount of information does need to pass through a small number of operational partners:

Where Your Information Is Processed

Some of the services listed above — including Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, and Meta Pixel — are operated by companies based outside Canada, and the information they collect may be stored or processed on servers in other countries, including the United States. Where this happens, your information may become subject to the laws of that jurisdiction. We choose service providers that maintain reasonable safeguards for personal information, regardless of where it's processed.

Data Security and Retention

We rely on standard technical safeguards to protect your information, including SSL encryption on our website and secure, reputable third-party providers for payment processing and data storage. Access to personal information internally is limited to staff who genuinely need it to do their jobs.

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, provide our services, meet legal and accounting requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Retention periods vary by the type of information involved — order and accounting records, for instance, are generally kept longer than marketing consent preferences. Once information is no longer needed for these purposes, it's securely deleted or anonymized.

No system is completely immune to risk, and we can't promise absolute security. If we experience a privacy breach that requires notification under applicable Canadian law, we will take the required steps to contain the incident, investigate it, and notify affected individuals and regulators as required.

Your Privacy Rights

Under applicable Canadian privacy legislation, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to access the personal information we hold about you.
  • The right to request corrections to information that's inaccurate or out of date.
  • The right to withdraw consent for any purpose based on consent, such as marketing communications.
  • The right to request deletion of your account or personal information where appropriate, subject to information we are required or permitted to retain by law or for legitimate business purposes.

To act on any of these, reach out using the contact details below — we'll respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. If a concern isn't resolved to your satisfaction after contacting us directly, you also have the right to bring it to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, the federal body responsible for privacy law oversight nationally.
