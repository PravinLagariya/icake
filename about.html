Our Story

Our Story: Baking Since 1962

Since 1962, Irresistible Cakes (iCakes) has been part of the GTA's celebration industry. What began as a single bakery has grown into multiple pickup locations across Toronto and the surrounding GTA, while cake production remains centralized in our 100% nut-free production facility.

This means cakes picked up from different locations come from the same kitchen and follow the same production standards. It has allowed the business to expand its reach while keeping the focus on consistency for more than six decades.

What Makes Our Cakes Different

Freshly Baked

Every cake is made fresh, with attention paid to taste, texture, and presentation.

Specialty Sponge & Custard

An Italian-style sponge and our signature custard form the base of most cakes, giving them their moist texture and flavour.

Dietary Options

Selected gluten-free, eggless, vegan, and lactose-free options are available across the range, generally as special orders.

Custom Designs

From photo cakes and edible figurines to detailed fondant work and full wedding designs, custom cakes are built around the occasion.

30+ Flavours

A wide flavour selection gives customers room to personalize a cake, including mixing flavours across tiers or layers for larger orders.

Quality & Dietary

Our Approach to Quality & Dietary Needs

Quality is built into the way the bakery operates. The team follows documented procedures for ingredient sourcing, food safety, and dietary handling, with quality control incorporated throughout production..
