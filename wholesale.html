Wholesale Cakes & Desserts in Toronto & the GTA

Irresistible Cakes (iCakes) is also a reputable wholesale supplier of premium sponge cakes and desserts for businesses across Toronto and the GTA, including restaurants, hotels, catering companies, bakeries, corporate events and other food businesses. Our wholesale range includes cakes, cupcakes, cookies, cake pops, dessert cups, pastries, tarts and our signature custard filling. We'd love to chat about your wholesale needs — our team will do its best to arrange pickup or delivery to your store or shop.
