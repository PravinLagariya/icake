GTA’s Premier Specialty Cake Manufacturer: Committed to Quality Since 1962

For over six decades, Irresistible Cakes (iCakes.ca) has been the Greater Toronto Area’s most trusted specialty cake manufacturer. We combine 63+ years of celebrated artistry with rigorous, baking engineering-backed quality control, operating from a 100% dedicated nut-free production facility to deliver premium, custom cakes and desserts for every GTA celebration. As one of the oldest specialty cake manufacturers in the region, we’ve proudly served thousands of customers and events—from weddings and birthdays to baptisms, communions, corporate milestones, holiday celebrations, and more.

Our Commitment to Auditable Quality & Dietary Safety

Our products are designed to be safe, inclusive, and delicious for all customers. For consumers with critical dietary requirements, our operational excellence provides measurable assurance:

  • 100% Dedicated Nut-Free Assurance: We operate exclusively from a dedicated nut-free production facility, providing a critical, auditable safety guarantee for all customers and partners. Learn More.
  • Engineering-Backed Quality Management: All operations are managed under the strict oversight of Alex Jasu, Business Manager and Head of the Quality Department, leveraging his background in Chemical and Quality Engineering to enforce strict Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for food safety.
  • Inclusive Options: We specialize in custom solutions including:
  • Lactose-free signature custards.
  • Gluten-free, Eggless (egg free) and Vegan cakes available upon request.
  • Fine Ingredients Policy: Our commitment ensures no trans fats (only healthy oils), gelatin, meat, or alcohol are used in any product formulation (except eggs).

Meet Our Team: Expertise, Experience, Authority

Our team’s diverse professional background ensures both creative artistry and technical compliance, establishing iCakes as an authoritative leader in specialized baking.

Our collective team of Management, Bakers and Decorators are professionals who uphold the strict standards of our 100% nut-free production facility, bringing decades of combined, first-hand experience to every custom order and wholesale order. Their expertise is demonstrated in every moist cake, intricate design, and specialized dietary offering (including our lactose-free custards, gluten-free, and eggless options), guaranteeing the technical perfection that makes every iCakes creation memorable.

Alex Jasu: Business Manager & Head of Quality Department

Alex Jasu, our Business Manager, doesn't just manage the business—he brings an essential technical edge to our kitchen. With a powerful background in Chemical Engineering, Quality Engineering, and Business Management, he ensures our baking is as precise as it is delicious.

  • He is instrumental in refining production workflows and ensuring the unwavering high standard of quality that has defined iCakes since 1962.
  • He established and maintains strict Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) to govern every aspect of our business—from ingredient sourcing and food safety to our crucial adherence to dietary restrictions.
  • As the head of our Quality Department, Alex ensures that any issue is immediately addressed with precise preventive actions, guaranteeing the technical perfection of every cake.

Milkias: Head Baker & Decorator
