Cake Flavours in Toronto

Over 35 flavours, built from recipes refined across more than 60 years of baking for GTA families. Every flavour on this menu is nut-free and lactose-free as standard, and most are available in eggless, gluten-free, dairy-free, or vegan versions. Whether you're ordering a birthday cake in Brampton, a wedding cake in Mississauga, or a celebration cake anywhere across the GTA, this is the full list of what's available.

Ingredients & Production Standards

Every flavour is made in a 100% nut-free facility using lactose-free milk and alcohol-free, gelatine-free recipes as standard. Products may contain traces of soya or sesame due to shared production.
