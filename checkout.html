Terms & Conditions

1. Custom Order & Design Agreement

All custom cake designs, photo prints, and color specifications submitted during checkout are final. Minor color variations may occur due to food-grade icing dyes and natural ingredients.

2. Cancellation & Refund Policy

Cancellations requested at least 48 hours prior to the scheduled pickup or delivery time are eligible for a full store credit or refund. Orders cancelled under 48 hours are non-refundable as preparation begins in advance.

3. Pickup & Delivery Terms

For in-store pickup, please present your order confirmation email. For doorstep delivery, please ensure someone is present during your selected time window. Re-delivery fees may apply if no one is available.

4. Dietary & Allergen Disclaimer

While we offer eggless, gluten-free, halal, and nut-free options, our kitchen handles dairy and wheat. If you have severe life-threatening allergies, please consult our staff prior to ordering.
