How to Choose the Perfect Birthday Cake

A birthday cake is the centrepiece of the party — but with so many sizes, finishes and flavours, choosing one can feel harder than it should. Here's a simple way to narrow it down, based on what GTA families order most.

1Start With the Guest Count

Size is the one decision you can't fix later, so settle it first. Count the people who will actually eat cake, then allow a little extra for second slices. A cake that looks right in a photo is sometimes wrong for the room — a 6-inch heart serves around 6–7 people, while a bigger party is better served by a larger round, a slab or a two-tier design.

If you're unsure, just tell the team your guest count and they'll recommend a size that fits.

2Pick a Design That Suits the Person

The best birthday cakes feel personal. Think about who's being celebrated and what they love, then match the style:

For kids, character or themed designs are hard to beat. For adults, photo cakes, vintage and sports designs are consistently popular.

3Choose a Flavour Everyone Will Enjoy

Design gets the attention, but flavour is what people talk about on the drive home. If the guest list spans several ages, French Vanilla is the safest pick — it's the most ordered flavour on the menu. For something brighter in summer, try Lemon Raspberry or Vanilla Fresh Strawberry. Chocolate lovers will be happy with Chocolate Chocolate, and Black Forest is a classic that holds up well over a long party.

4Check Dietary Needs Early

Before you fall in love with a design, check that it works for every guest. Everything at iCakes is made in a 100% nut-free facility, and every cake is halal, alcohol-free and gelatine-free as standard. Lactose-free milk is used across most flavours, and eggless and gluten-free versions are available on special order.
