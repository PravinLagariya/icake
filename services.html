Custom Cakes in Toronto & GTA Made Fresh Since 1962

For more than 60 years, Irresistible Cakes (iCakes) has been making custom cakes for birthdays, weddings, graduations, anniversaries, baby showers, and special occasions. You can select a custom cake as per your preferred design or a ready-made one for fast pickup.

Every cake is baked fresh and made in our 100% dedicated nut-free facility — so every guest can celebrate safely. We also offer Eggless (egg-free cakes), lactose-free, and gluten-free cakes, with halal-friendly options available across our full range. iCakes makes it easy to find the perfect cake for every celebration.
