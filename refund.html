Return & Refund Policy of Irresistible Cakes (iCakes)

Home /Return & Refund Policy

Thank You for Choosing Irresistible Cakes

At Irresistible Cakes, every cake is freshly baked and often customized specifically for your celebration. Because our products are made to order and are perishable, we have established this Return & Refund Policy to ensure fairness for both our customers and our team. By placing an order online, by phone, or in person, you agree to the terms outlined below.


Order Cancellations & Changes

We understand that plans can change. If you need to cancel or modify your order, please contact us as soon as possible.

Cancellation Notice Requirements

Please provide advance notice based on your order type to receive a cancellation.

Regular Cakes & Products

(Up to 2-Tier Cakes)
Minimum Notice 5 Days

Cancel at least 5 days before your scheduled pickup or delivery.

Large Celebration & Wedding Cakes

Minimum Notice 10 Days

Cancel at least 10 days before your scheduled pickup or delivery.

What Happens If You Cancel on Time?

If your cancellation request is received within the notice period listed above, we will issue a store credit for the full value of your order.


Store Credit Benefits

  • Can be used toward a future order
  • Can be transferred to another celebration date
  • Issued directly to the customer on file

Order Cancellations & Changes

We understand that plans can change. If you need to cancel or modify your order, please contact us as soon as possible.

Late Cancellations

Once production has started, ingredients, labour, and preparation time cannot be recovered.

For this reason:

  • Orders cancelled less than 5 days before pickup or delivery for regular cakes are non-refundable.
  • Orders cancelled less than 10 days before pickup or delivery for large celebration cakes or wedding cakes are non-refundable.
  • Store credits are not available for late cancellations.

Order Changes

We will do our best to accommodate design, flavour, or decoration changes whenever possible.

Please note:

  • Changes should ideally be requested at least 14 days before the event date.
  • Requests made closer to the event date may not be possible due to production schedules.
  • Any approved changes must be confirmed by our team.

Custom Cake Expectations

Every custom cake is handcrafted by our decorators and made to order.

While we strive to match reference images and approved designs as closely as possible, slight variations in colour, decorative details, fondant finish, texture, and artistic interpretation may occur. These variations are normal in custom cake production and do not qualify for a refund.

Damage, Defects & Quality Concerns

We take great pride in every cake we create and encourage customers to inspect their order immediately at pickup or delivery.

If you believe there is an issue with your order, please contact us within one hour of receiving the cake.

To help us properly review your concern, customers may be asked to provide:

  • Clear photographs of the cake
  • Photographs of any damage
  • Images of the packaging if applicable
  • Order details and a description of the issue

Claims submitted without supporting photographs may not qualify for a refund, credit, or replacement.

Products That Have Been Consumed

Because cakes and baked goods are perishable food products, refunds or store credits cannot be issued for products that have been substantially consumed.

If you have concerns regarding quality, flavour, design, or condition, please contact us before consuming the product whenever possible.

Third-Party Delivery Disclaimer

Some orders within the Greater Toronto Area may be delivered through third-party delivery services.

While we take every precaution to package cakes safely, Irresistible Cakes cannot be responsible for delays, mishandling, damage, or loss caused by third-party delivery providers.

Customers are encouraged to inspect their cake immediately upon delivery and report any concerns as soon as possible.

For the highest level of care and control, we recommend:

  • Store pickup, or
  • Specialized cake delivery services arranged directly through Irresistible Cakes when available

Refunds & Store Credits

Refund Method

Approved refunds will only be processed to the original payment method used when placing the order.

This may include:

  • Visa
  • Mastercard
  • American Express
  • Debit Card
  • E-Transfer

Processing Time

Once approved, refunds generally appear within 3–5 business days, depending on your financial institution.

Store Credits

Approved store credits will be issued to the customer associated with the order and may be used toward future purchases in accordance with company policy.

Cake Stand & Equipment Deposits

If you rent cake stands, pillars, columns, or other display equipment from Irresistible Cakes, your security deposit will be refunded when:

  • The equipment is returned in good condition
  • The equipment is returned within 7 days of the event
All returns must be made to our Main Woodbridge location (4040 Steeles Ave West, Woodbridge, ON) unless otherwise arranged.
