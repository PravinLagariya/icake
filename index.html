Our Story

A GTA Cake Bakery That Has Earned Its Reputation — One Order at a Time

Since 1962, one standard has guided every cake that leaves this bakery: it should be worth putting the name on.

That means a fully nut-free facility — not just select products. Lactose-free milk is used as a core ingredient, not a last-minute substitution. Every order carries a QC Passed stamp before it leaves the building, following the same standard operating procedures refined over six decades.

The GTA has grown and diversified. So has our range of custom cakes. The standard behind it hasn't changed.

QC Passed stamp on every box before pickup

The Flavours

More Than 35 Flavours — Because the Cake Has to Taste as Good as It Looks

Design gets the attention. Flavour is what people talk about on the drive home. Every cake at iCakes is made with real ingredients — fresh strawberries, 100% lemon juice, Brazilian café custard, rich cocoa — not flavouring concentrates or artificial substitutes.

From decades-old classics to the flavours guests request after trying them at someone else's celebration, the range covers the full spread of tastes and occasions.

French Vanilla

Moist vanilla sponge with white French Vanilla custard. The most consistently ordered flavour on the menu.

Lemon Raspberry

Real lemon juice with raspberry custard — the flavour guests most often request on repeat.

Black Forest

Chocolate sponge, whipped cream, sweet cherries. A classic that holds well over long gatherings.

Café Latte

Italian vanilla sponge with Brazilian coffee custard. Popular for adult milestone celebrations.

Green Apple Cinnamon

Vanilla sponge with green apple custard and cinnamon. Distinctive and consistently surprising.

Vanilla Fresh Strawberry

Fresh whipped cream and real sliced strawberries. The first choice for spring and summer parties.

Chocolate Cha Cha

Milk chocolate sponge with Brazilian café custard, for guests who want chocolate with more depth.

Eggless versions are available across most flavours. Gluten-free is available across select flavours. All 35+ options and their dietary variants are listed on the flavours page.

Dietary Options

Baked for Everyone at the Table

Dietary needs shift from one celebration to the next — iCakes is built to cover them, not as exceptions, but as part of how the bakery was designed to operate.

100% Nut-Free Facility

The entire baking environment — every surface, tool, and ingredient — is completely nut-free. Not a nut-free product from a shared kitchen, but a nut-free bakery where nuts have never been present. Our allergen-safe cakes are trusted across Brampton and the wider GTA.

Learn more

Lactose-Free

Lactose-free, dairy-free-friendly milk is used as a core ingredient across most flavours — built into the original recipe rather than offered as a substitution. Most guests with lactose intolerance need no modification to the standard order.

Learn more

Eggless & Vegan

Eggless cakes are made on dedicated egg-free recipes across 13+ flavours, not a standard recipe with eggs removed. Vegan cakes are also available on special order across the GTA — mark the requirement at order time.

Learn more

Gluten-Free

A dedicated gluten-free sponge base, not a flour substitution applied to the standard recipe. Available across select flavours on special order — confirm the requirement when placing your order.

Learn more

Halal Friendly

All products are halal, alcohol-free, and gelatine-free as standard, including every flavouring agent. The vanilla used across the range contains no alcohol, so each product qualifies without a separate request.

Learn more
