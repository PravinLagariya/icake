Customer Policy

Return & Cancellation Policy

All of our cakes are custom-made to order, using fresh ingredients and careful, hands-on decoration work that often begins well before your pickup or delivery date. Because each cake is made specifically for one customer and cannot be resold once we have started working on it, whether you are eligible for a refund, credit, or exchange depends on timing — the earlier you let us know about a change, the more options you will have.

Terms Used in This Policy

A few terms come up throughout this policy:

  • Order means any custom cake you purchase from us, whether you order online, by phone, or in person.
  • Event Date means the pickup or delivery date we confirm when you place your order.
  • Deposit means a partial payment made when you place your order, in cases where we don't require full payment upfront.
  • Store Credit means a credit balance you can use toward a future order with us, instead of a cash refund.

1. Order Cancellations

Whether you're eligible for a refund, credit, or transfer depends on two things: how soon after placing your order you cancel, and how close your Event Date is at that time.

Cancelling Within 24 Hours of Placing Your Order

If your Event Date is still 7 or more days away, cancelling within 24 hours of placing your order gets you a full refund — 100% of what you paid, back to your original payment method.

If your Event Date is less than 7 days away, this 24-hour cancellation period doesn't apply. Orders this close to their Event Date go into production right after they're confirmed, so ingredients are already being sourced and prepared before the first 24 hours are even up. Because of this, these orders can't be cancelled or refunded, no matter how quickly you get in touch with us.

Cancelling at Least 7 Days Before Your Event Date

Once the first 24 hours have passed, a different set of options applies. Cancel at least 7 days before your Event Date, and you can choose one of three options:

  • A 70% refund of your deposit or payment, back to your original payment method.
  • A full transfer of your payment toward a new cake order.
  • Store credit for 100% of your payment, valid for one year from the date it's issued, usable toward any future order.

Cancelling Less Than 7 Days Before Your Event Date

By this point, most of the work is already underway — ingredients purchased, decoration plans finalized, and production scheduled. Cancellations made this close to your Event Date are non-refundable and non-creditable, and the full order amount is still due, whether or not the cake is picked up.

2. If We Cannot Fulfill Your Order

Sometimes, things happen on our end that are outside our control.

This can include:

  • An ingredient shortage
  • An unexpected closure
  • Illness affecting our team
  • Other similar circumstances beyond our control

If this happens, here's what we'll do:

  • Contact you as early as possible
  • Offer you a full refund
  • Where possible, help arrange an alternative that still meets your Event Date

This applies no matter how close your Event Date is. It's a different situation from cancelling your own order, and we handle it separately.

3. Pickup, Delivery & Handling

Order Collection

Orders need to be picked up on the scheduled date. If an order isn't picked up, it's forfeited — this means no refund or credit is given, since the cake has already been made and can't be resold.

Customer Pickup and Transport

Once you or someone on your behalf picks up the cake, responsibility for its condition passes to you. We're not liable for damage that happens after pickup, including from transport, mishandling, or storage that doesn't follow our instructions.

To help the cake travel well:

  • Keep it flat and stable, avoiding sudden braking, turns, or tilting.
  • Shield it from direct sun and heat, and limit time outside refrigeration.
  • Refrigerate it promptly on arrival, following the storage guidance given at pickup.

Delivery Arranged by Us

If we arrange delivery directly, rather than you collecting the order yourself, responsibility for the cake's condition stays with us until it's handed over at the delivery address.

If your delivered cake arrives damaged, please contact us right away so we can look into it and make things right.

4. Quality Concerns

If something about your cake doesn't meet expectations, we'd rather hear about it than have you walk away unhappy.

Report it quickly

Contact us within 24 hours of your scheduled pickup or delivery time — the sooner we know, the more we can do.

Send photos first

A clear photo of your concern helps our quality team start looking into it right away, especially if you reach out after business hours or before you're able to bring the cake to the store.

Bring back what's left

For us to properly look into the concern, at least 75% of the unconsumed cake needs to come back to our bakery within that same 24-hour time period.

Every situation is a little different, so how we resolve it depends on what our team finds once they've reviewed the returned cake.

Allergen and Dietary Accuracy

If your order was placed with specific dietary requirements — nut-free, halal, lactose-free, eggless, vegan, or gluten-free, for example — and you believe what you received doesn't match what was confirmed when you ordered, contact us immediately, before the cake is eaten.

Given how seriously we take dietary accuracy, concerns like this are treated as a priority and reviewed separately from the usual 24-hour period for quality concerns.

6. General Terms

This policy applies to every order placed with us — online, by phone, or in person. Store credit issued under this policy is valid for one year from the date it's issued and can't be exchanged for cash.

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, the update applies to orders placed after the change — it won't change the terms of an order you've already confirmed.

If any part of this policy turns out to be invalid or unenforceable under the law, the rest of the policy still applies in full. Nothing here is meant to limit or override any rights you have under Canadian or Ontario consumer protection law.

Where events outside our control — like severe weather or supply disruptions — affect our ability to prepare your order, we handle it the same way as described above, under "If We Cannot Fulfill Your Order."

If instead you're the one who can't collect your cake because of something on your end, including weather, please contact us as early as possible so we can work out the best next step together.

Questions before you order? Contact us — we're glad to walk through anything in this policy before you commit to a date.
